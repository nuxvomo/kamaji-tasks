Kamaji Tasks

Kamaji Tasks is a private, personal-use tool that lets Jim Kincaid's personal assistant manage the Kincaid family's own Google Tasks lists: creating, updating and completing tasks.

It is not offered to the public, and only accounts belonging to the Kincaid family are connected to it.
